Architects of Stakeholder Capitalism

An encyclopedia of the World Economic Forum's Global Leaders for Tomorrow (1993–2003) and Young Global Leaders (2005–2026).

Between 1993 and 2026 the World Economic Forum named 4,299 people to two leadership
programmes. This is the record of who they were, what the roster said about them, and what
became of them — every name, none omitted.

## How to read an entry

Entries are grouped **by country**, alphabetically. Within a country they run by **class year**,
earliest first, then by **name**. Every entry states its programme and class year.

> **On country.** Country is the value the roster carried *at the time of listing*. It is not a
> citizenship field, and the research did not re-derive it — "Canada" means the roster filed that
> person under Canada, nothing more. Variant spellings of the same country have been merged so
> that a country appears once; every merge is declared in the notes at the end of each volume.

Each entry carries its sources. Where a source was thin, contested, or absent, the entry says
so rather than guessing.

## The numbers

**1,164 + 3,135 = 4,299 entries** across the two volumes. That is the complete set.

**74 entries are marked deceased — but In Memoriam lists 72 people.** The gap is not an error.
Two of the dead appear in both programmes, counted once as people and twice as entries.

**There is no 2004 class.** 2004 was the transition year between the two programmes, and no
roster for it was found. The arithmetic bears this out: eleven GLT class years plus twenty-two
YGL class years account for every year covered, with 2004 absent from both.

## Method

Research ran in **batches of twenty-five listed graduates at a time**, worked in order until the
rosters were exhausted. No name was skipped, and no name was dropped for being hard to trace —
an entry that returned little says that plainly instead of disappearing.

Two kinds of source sit behind every entry:

- **The World Economic Forum's own published rosters**, which supply the name, the class year,
  the country, and the title as listed at the time.
- **Open-web research against each name**, which supplies what happened next.

Where the two disagree, the disagreement is recorded rather than resolved silently.

In Memoriam was compiled **7 September 2026** and reflects deaths known at that date. The
biographical entries carry their own "as of" dates where currency matters.

## Accuracy and corrections

This is a compilation of public record. It is not an allegation about any individual, and no
entry asserts more than its sources support.

Research at this scale, across 4,299 people and thirty-three years, will contain errors. Names
collide, rosters carry typos, and people change countries, employers and surnames.

**If you are listed here and something is wrong, please open an issue on this repository.**
Corrections are welcome from anyone, and will be carried into the next revision. Please say
which volume, which page, and what the correct information is — a source helps but is not
required.
